Email Welcome Sequence

Email Marketing · 3 emails · Fictional brand: Fern & Field Skincare

The strategy A new subscriber is warm but skeptical. Email 1 delivers the promised discount and sets the brand voice. Email 2 builds trust with the founder story and social proof. Email 3 creates urgency to convert before the welcome discount expires.

Email 2 — The Story

Subject: Why I started mixing creams in my kitchen

Preheader: It started with my daughter's eczema. It became something bigger.

Hi there,

Three years ago, my daughter broke out in a rash from a "gentle" big-brand moisturizer. I flipped the bottle around and couldn't pronounce half the ingredients.

So I started making my own. Oatmeal, calendula, shea butter. Stuff from the garden and the farmer's market.

Her skin cleared up. Then my neighbor asked for a jar. Then her sister. Then the whole street.

That's how Fern & Field started — in a kitchen, not a boardroom.

Every formula is still made in small batches. Every ingredient is still one I'd put on my own kid's skin.
